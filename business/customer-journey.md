# Customer Journey Map for Terrain-Lock

## 1. Aware
- **Trigger Event**: A drone operator experiences GPS jamming during a critical mission.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about alternatives to GPS; misinformation about the reliability of non-GPS navigation systems.
- **User Emotions**: Frustration, confusion, concern for safety and mission success.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Educational content on the limitations of GPS and the benefits of terrain recognition and inertial measurement; testimonials from early adopters.
- **Metric**: Number of website visits to educational resources; engagement rate on social media posts about GPS jamming.

## 2. Consider
- **Trigger Event**: Researching solutions to GPS jamming issues; comparing products.
- **Friction Points**: Overwhelming amount of information; difficulty in understanding technical specifications; skepticism about effectiveness.
- **User Emotions**: Hopeful yet cautious; desire for reassurance and validation.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Clear comparison charts; case studies demonstrating successful use cases; free webinars or Q&A sessions with experts.
- **Metric**: Number of product comparisons viewed; time spent on product pages.

## 3. Try
- **Trigger Event**: User decides to test the terrain-lock system in a controlled environment.
- **Friction Points**: Initial setup complexity; uncertainty about integration with existing drone systems.
- **User Emotions**: Excitement mixed with anxiety; eagerness to see results but worried about potential failure.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Comprehensive onboarding support; step-by-step setup guides; responsive customer support during trial.
- **Metric**: Trial conversion rate; user feedback score on setup experience.

## 4. Adopt
- **Trigger Event**: Successful trial leading to confidence in the product.
- **Friction Points**: Long-term commitment concerns; potential hidden costs; integration with other systems.
- **User Emotions**: Satisfaction; relief from previous GPS issues; pride in using advanced technology.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Loyalty programs; personalized follow-up to ensure satisfaction; community building through user forums.
- **Metric**: Customer retention rate; Net Promoter Score (NPS).

## 5. Expand
- **Trigger Event**: User seeks to enhance their drone fleet or operations.
- **Friction Points**: Uncertainty about scaling the solution; potential compatibility issues with new drones.
- **User Emotions**: Ambition; desire for growth; cautious optimism.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Bundled offers for multiple units; tailored solutions for different drone models; ongoing education on advanced features.
- **Metric**: Number of additional units sold; upsell conversion rate.
