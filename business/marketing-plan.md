# Marketing Plan for Terrain-Lock
## Positioning
### Primary Positioning Statement
Terrain-Lock: "Unshakeable Navigation for Uninterrupted Drone Operations"

### Alternative Positioning Statements
1. Terrain-Lock: "GPS Jamming-Proof Navigation for Drones"
2. Terrain-Lock: "Terrain-Recognition Powered Navigation for Secure Drone Operations"
3. Terrain-Lock: "Inertial Measurement and Terrain Recognition for Reliable Drone Navigation"

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
### Persona 1: Alex Chen, Drone Operations Manager
- Daily Job: Overseeing drone operations for a construction company
- Budget: $50,000 - $100,000 per year for drone operations and maintenance
- Pain Points: GPS jamming, navigation errors, and downtime

### Persona 2: Maya Patel, Drone Pilot
- Daily Job: Conducting aerial surveys and inspections for a government agency
- Budget: $20,000 - $50,000 per year for drone equipment and training
- Pain Points: GPS jamming, navigation errors, and difficulty in navigating complex terrain

### Persona 3: Jack Lee, Drone System Integrator
- Daily Job: Integrating drones with various systems for industrial clients
- Budget: $100,000 - $500,000 per year for system integration and maintenance
- Pain Points: Integration challenges, navigation errors, and downtime

## Channels
### Top 3 Channels with CAC Estimate
1. **Drone Industry Events** (CAC: $500 - $1,000 per lead)
	* Attend drone conferences and trade shows to showcase Terrain-Lock
	* Network with industry professionals and gather feedback
2. **Targeted Online Advertising** (CAC: $100 - $500 per lead)
	* Run targeted ads on LinkedIn, Twitter, and drone-related websites
	* Focus on reaching drone operations managers, pilots, and system integrators
3. **Influencer Marketing** (CAC: $500 - $2,000 per lead)
	* Partner with influential drone industry thought leaders and pilots
	* Showcase Terrain-Lock's capabilities and benefits through sponsored content

## Content Cadence
### Week-by-Week Content Plan
Week 1-2: Introduction to Terrain-Lock and its benefits
Week 3-4: Technical deep dive into terrain recognition and inertial measurement
Week 5-6: Case studies and success stories from early adopters
Week 7-8: Comparison with traditional GPS navigation systems
Week 9-10: Product updates and new feature announcements
Week 11-12: User testimonials and reviews

## Launch Milestones
### D-30 (30 Days Before Launch)
- Finalize marketing materials, including website, social media, and advertising assets
- Establish influencer partnerships and begin content creation

### D-0 (Launch Day)
- Launch Terrain-Lock website and marketing campaigns
- Host a webinar to introduce Terrain-Lock and its benefits

### D+30 (30 Days After Launch)
- Analyze launch metrics and adjust marketing strategies as needed
- Begin content creation for the next quarter

### D+90 (90 Days After Launch)
- Review progress towards $MRR target and adjust pricing or packaging as needed
- Plan for future product updates and feature enhancements

## Success Metrics
### DAU (Daily Active Users)
- Target: 100 DAU within the first 30 days
- Metric: Track daily active users through website analytics and user engagement

### $MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) Target
- Target: $10,000 MRR within the first 90 days
- Metric: Track monthly recurring revenue through sales and customer data
